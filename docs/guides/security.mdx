<Warning>
Antech is experimental and under independent cryptanalysis. Do not treat passing benchmarks or SDK packaging as a security endorsement.
</Warning>

## Do

- Prefer Argon2id for production until review results are published
- Store only `$antech$v2$` strings
- Use TLS and constant-time auth responses
- Keep SDKs on the published FFI — no reimplemented crypto

## Don’t

- Ship custom graphs as “compatible” without new vectors
- Log passwords or salts in cleartext
- Disable verification errors as soft failures

Review package: `research/security-review/`.
