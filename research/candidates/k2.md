# Variant K2 (historical)

Research fork with four parents per step for a steeper TMTO curve.

```text
MixedBlock = Block[Addr_1] ^ Block[Addr_2] ^ Block[Addr_3] ^ Block[Addr_4]
S_{i+1} = ARX(S_i, MixedBlock)
```

Measured on the early campaign host at 16 MiB: defender p50 ~112 ms, 16-core attacker ~18.8 g/s, TMTO ~13.93× at half memory (much higher at 12.5%). Not the production engine; production uses combined-frontier (`g=3`).
